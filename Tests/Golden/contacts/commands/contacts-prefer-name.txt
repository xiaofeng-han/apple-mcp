usage: apple-contacts-mcp contacts prefer-name CARD

Make this card give its linked contact the name.

  CARD                unlink: a card (only that one) or a contact (all its cards); prefer_name, prefer_photo: the card
  --format text|json  Print the answer as text (default) or JSON
  --json json|-       Fields as a JSON object (- reads it from stdin); flags override them
  -h, --help          Show this help

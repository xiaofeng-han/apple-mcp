usage: apple-contacts-mcp groups members [GROUP] [flags]

A group's or smart list's members (--nested adds its nested groups'), and its distribution list.

  GROUP               A group or smart list (name or id): list its members
  --[no-]nested       With group: include the members of its nested groups
  --format text|json  Print the answer as text (default) or JSON
  --json json|-       Fields as a JSON object (- reads it from stdin); flags override them
  -h, --help          Show this help

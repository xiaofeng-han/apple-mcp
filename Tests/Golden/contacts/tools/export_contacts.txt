Export contacts: Writes contacts to a .vcf file, as Contacts' File > Export vCard does: chosen ones, a group's or smart list's, those matching a query, the My Card (Share My Card), or all. vCard 3.0 (or 2.1), with notes and photos unless turned off.

Input schema: {"additionalProperties":false,"properties":{"group":{"description":"A group's or smart list's members (name or id)","type":"string"},"ids":{"description":"These contacts","items":{"type":"string"},"type":"array"},"my_card":{"description":"The My Card","type":"boolean"},"notes":{"description":"Include notes (default true)","type":"boolean"},"photos":{"description":"Include photos (default true)","type":"boolean"},"query":{"description":"Contacts matching these words, as find_contacts","type":"string"},"to":{"description":"Absolute path of the .vcf file to write (it must not exist)","type":"string"},"version":{"description":"Default 3.0","enum":["3.0","2.1"],"type":"string"}},"required":["to"],"type":"object"}

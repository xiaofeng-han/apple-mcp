Edit a journal entry: Changes an entry in one call: its title, its text (body rewrites it all; replace swaps exact Markdown from read_entry, pairs applied in order; append adds Markdown at the end), its date, bookmark or journal. Only what changes is written; the rest keeps its formatting. An entry changed since its version was read is refused.

Input schema: {"additionalProperties":false,"properties":{"append":{"description":"Markdown to add at the end of the text","type":"string"},"body":{"description":"The whole text, as Markdown","type":"string"},"bookmarked":{"description":"Bookmark (true) or not (false)","type":"boolean"},"date":{"description":"2026-10-21T09:30, or 2026-10-21","type":"string"},"id":{"description":"Entry id","type":"string"},"journal":{"description":"Move it to this journal (name or id); \"\" takes it out of its journal","type":"string"},"replace":{"description":"Replacements in the text","items":{"additionalProperties":false,"properties":{"all":{"description":"Replace every occurrence","type":"boolean"},"new":{"description":"Markdown","type":"string"},"old":{"description":"Exact Markdown; must be unique unless all","type":"string"}},"required":["old","new"],"type":"object"},"type":"array"},"title":{"description":"The new title; \"\" for none","type":"string"},"version":{"description":"The version read_entry or find_entries gave","type":"string"}},"required":["id","version"],"type":"object"}

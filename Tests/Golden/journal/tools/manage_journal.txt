Create, change or delete a journal: Creates a journal, changes one's name, color or icon, or deletes one. Deleting moves its entries to another journal (move_entries_to), leaves them in All Entries only (the default), or with delete_entries moves them to Recently Deleted too (ask the user first; expected_entries is how many it holds). The default journal cannot be changed.

Input schema: {"additionalProperties":false,"properties":{"action":{"description":"What to do","enum":["create","update","delete"],"type":"string"},"color":{"description":"#RRGGBB, or red, orange, yellow, green, mint, teal, cyan, blue, indigo, purple, pink, brown, gray","type":"string"},"delete_entries":{"description":"delete: move its entries to Recently Deleted too","type":"boolean"},"expected_entries":{"description":"delete with delete_entries: how many entries it holds","type":"integer"},"icon":{"description":"An emoji, or an SF Symbol name such as book.closed","type":"string"},"journal":{"description":"New journal name for create; journal name or id otherwise","type":"string"},"move_entries_to":{"description":"delete: the journal to move its entries to (name or id)","type":"string"},"new_name":{"description":"update: the new name","type":"string"}},"required":["action","journal"],"type":"object"}

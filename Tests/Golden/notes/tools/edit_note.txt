Edit a note: Changes a note in one call: replace (exact Markdown from read_note, pairs applied in order), insert (Markdown at the end, the start, or under a heading), or rewrite (the whole note); check, uncheck and checklist tick, untick, sort or clear checklist items; pinned pins it. Only what changes is written; the rest keeps all its formatting. A note changed since its version was read is refused.

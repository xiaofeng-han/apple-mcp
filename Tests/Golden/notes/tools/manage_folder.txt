Manage a folder: Creates (folder is the new path; with tags, a smart folder of the notes with them), renames, moves (into another folder, or the top level), deletes or exports a folder. delete takes an empty folder, or with recursive and expected_notes everything in it (the notes go to Recently Deleted). export writes it and its folders as Markdown with the original attachments, to a new folder or .zip. Renaming or moving a shared folder needs allow_shared, after asking the user.

Input schema: {"additionalProperties":false,"properties":{"action":{"description":"What to do","enum":["create","rename","move","delete","export"],"type":"string"},"allow_shared":{"description":"The user agreed to renaming or moving a shared folder","type":"boolean"},"expected_notes":{"description":"delete with recursive: the notes you saw in it and its folders","type":"integer"},"folder":{"description":"Path or id; for create, the new folder's path","type":"string"},"into":{"description":"For move: the folder to put it in; empty for the top level","type":"string"},"match":{"description":"For a smart folder: notes with any of the tags (default) or all of them","enum":["any","all"],"type":"string"},"name":{"description":"New name, for rename","type":"string"},"recursive":{"description":"delete: also everything in it","type":"boolean"},"tags":{"description":"For create: make a smart folder showing the notes with these tags","items":{"type":"string"},"type":"array"},"to":{"description":"For export: a new folder or .zip path","type":"string"}},"required":["action","folder"],"type":"object"}
